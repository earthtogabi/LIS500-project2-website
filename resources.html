Implicit Bias in Technology and Algorithms

Implicit bias refers to the unconscious attitudes and stereotypes that can shape our decisions and actions without us realizing it. For first-year students, this concept is important because bias does not only exist in personal interactions—it also shows up in the technologies we use every day.

From social media recommendation systems to artificial intelligence tools like facial recognition, algorithms can unintentionally inherit and amplify the biases present in their training data. This means that the design of technology can influence fairness, opportunity, and even safety.

By learning about implicit bias in technology, students can begin to critically question how systems are built, who benefits from them, and who might be excluded or harmed. This awareness is the first step toward building a more inclusive digital future.
